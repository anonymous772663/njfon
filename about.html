01 — WHO WE ARE
WE ARE

NJFON.

Neymar Jr Fans of Nepal is a supporter community created for people who share a genuine connection with Neymar Jr and the beautiful game.

For us, being a fan is not simply about following one player. It is about celebrating the creativity, joy, passion, and unique identity that Brazilian football has given to the world. From legendary Brazilian players to the stars of today, every generation carries the spirit of Jogo Bonito. Neymar Jr represents an important part of that journey, inspiring millions with his skill, imagination, and fearless style of play. Beyond football, NJFON also believes in the power of unity and social responsibility. By bringing together players from national and international levels, we aim to encourage and inspire greater participation in meaningful social initiatives and community activities. NJFON brings this shared passion together — a community where Neymar fans, football lovers, and players can connect, celebrate the game, contribute to society, and create a positive impact beyond the pitch.

02 — WHERE IT ALL BEGAN

It started in 2017.

A simple beginning became a story worth continuing.

2017THE BEGINNING

In 2017, NJFON began with a simple idea: bring Neymar Jr supporters in Nepal together.

Founded in 2017, NJFON began with a simple idea — to bring Neymar Jr supporters together through a shared love for football. What started as a connection between people who admired his creativity, individuality and style of play gradually grew into a community with its own identity.

Over the years, NJFON has continued to grow alongside its supporters. What began as a shared admiration for a footballer became an opportunity for fans across Nepal to connect, communicate, and experience their passion together. From following matches and sharing conversations to coming together through community activities, NJFON has continued to build on the idea that football can bring people closer.

Today, NJFON represents more than a name or a fan identity. It is a community shaped by years of shared experiences, new connections, and a common passion for Neymar Jr and football — continuing the journey that began in 2017.

05 — OUR GOALS

Build something
that lasts.

Our goal is not simply to grow bigger. It is to grow with meaning.

01To promote the football culture, sporting spirit, and positive values of Brazil Football and Neymar Jr. in Nepal.
02 To actively contribute to the development, promotion, and expansion of football in Nepal.
03To promote discipline, teamwork, leadership, sportsmanship, and social responsibility among young people, inspired by Brazilian football.
04To organize social service programs such as blood donation, sanitation campaigns, tree plantation, relief distribution, and support for communities in need..
05To collaborate with government and non-government organizations, football clubs, businesses, brands, and corporate partners to conduct impactful programs.
06To strengthen football relations between Nepal and Brazil and promote collaboration and knowledge exchange with the international football community.
07 To build a transparent, responsible, and sustainable organization that makes meaningful contributions to sports and society.
08To support financially disadvantaged students and young people by providing educational, sporting, and essential resources to help develop their potential.
09To identify, train, and provide opportunities to talented players and teams, helping them progress from local to national and international levels.
10 To regularly organize football tournaments, training programs, fan activities, friendly matches, and community initiatives.
